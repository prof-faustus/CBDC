# CBDC research and test platform

Research and test-only implementation of a single-currency central-bank payment platform, with a treasury sub-area for controlled issuance and retirement and value-conserving denomination splitting and merging.

This repository is being initialized for staged research and review. No legal issuer authority, real money, production readiness, network deployment, performance certification or regulatory approval is implied. Synthetic test units are not US dollars or claims on any government.

Implementation and source-grounded research will be proposed on a review branch. No open-source licence has been selected for project-authored code; third-party materials retain their own terms.
