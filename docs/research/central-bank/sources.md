## CB-005

- Publisher: International Monetary Fund, Working Paper 2020/254
- Published: 2020-11-20
- Status: Staff working paper; authors' views, not an IMF legal ruling
- Relevant location: Introduction; paragraph 16; central-bank-law and monetary-law analysis
